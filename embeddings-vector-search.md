# Embeddings and Vector Search Interview Questions

1. Walk through how embeddings and similarity search work. How do representation choice, distance metrics, and approximate nearest-neighbor indexes affect results?
2. Pinecone vs. Chroma vs. Weaviate: which would you choose, and why? Compare deployment requirements, scale, filtering, hybrid search, operational burden, and cost.
3. When does hybrid retrieval (vector plus keyword search) outperform pure vector search? How would you combine and evaluate the results?

For corpus-scale design and retrieval troubleshooting, see the [RAG question set](RAG/readme.md).
